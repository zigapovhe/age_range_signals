# Regulatory status

Last updated November 23, 2026.

These laws keep changing, but the advice stays the same: keep the plugin in your app and rely on what it returns at runtime, instead of hard-coding which regions are live.

> **Google Play is going worldwide.** Google says age-signals 0.0.6, used by this plugin since 0.11.0, will return responses for all users by December 11, 2026. Plugin versions 0.8.0 to 0.10.1 use 0.0.4, which keeps returning responses in selected regions: Australia, Brazil, Canada, and the US states Alabama, California and Texas ([Google docs](https://developer.android.com/google/play/age-signals/overview), [0.0.4 docs](https://developer.android.com/google/play/age-signals/v4/overview), [by region](https://developer.android.com/distribute/age-appropriate-experiences/regional-protections)). Where no law requires sharing, users choose whether to share, and anyone whose Play setting is "Ask before sharing" can see the prompt. To skip the prompt there, see [Regional Eligibility](README.md#regional-eligibility).

- **Brazil (Lei 15.211, Digital ECA):** In force since March 17, 2026, when Play started returning age signals for users in Brazil ([Google docs](https://developer.android.com/google/play/age-signals/v4/overview)). From January 2027, sharing in Brazil is mandatory on Play ([Google docs](https://developer.android.com/distribute/age-appropriate-experiences/regional-protections)). On Apple's side, since February 24, 2026 the App Store blocks users in Brazil from downloading 18+ apps unless they're a confirmed adult. Google Play also blocks users it identifies as minors from downloading apps rated 18+ there ([Google docs](https://developer.android.com/distribute/age-appropriate-experiences/regional-protections)). Apps that declare loot boxes in the age rating questionnaire are rated 18+ in the Brazil store. [Law](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2025/lei/L15211.htm) · [Google docs](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6223646?hl=en#digital_eca_requirements) · [Apple News](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=f5zj08ey)
- **Australia:** Apple points developers to the Declared Age Range API as a signal of a user's age here. Since February 24, 2026, Apple blocks users in Australia from downloading 18+ apps unless they're a confirmed adult. Google Play also blocks users it identifies as minors from downloading apps rated 18+ there ([Google docs](https://developer.android.com/distribute/age-appropriate-experiences/regional-protections)). This is separate from the [Social Media Minimum Age Act](https://www.esafety.gov.au/about-us/industry-regulation/social-media-age-restrictions) (in effect since December 10, 2025) and from App Store content *ratings*, which this plugin doesn't handle. eSafety's [Age-Restricted Material Codes](https://www.esafety.gov.au/industry/codes/register-online-industry-codes-standards) also require some app stores to check age before an 18+ download, with some of those checks required from September 9, 2026 ([fact sheet](https://www.esafety.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-04/Age-restricted-Material-Codes-fact-sheet-April2026.pdf)). Like content ratings, that store check is outside this plugin. [Apple News](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=f5zj08ey)
- **Singapore:** Apple points developers to the Declared Age Range API as a signal of a user's age here. Since February 24, 2026, Apple blocks users in Singapore from downloading 18+ apps unless they're a confirmed adult. Google Play also blocks users it identifies as minors from downloading apps rated 18+ there ([Google docs](https://developer.android.com/distribute/age-appropriate-experiences/regional-protections)). [Apple News](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=f5zj08ey)
- **Texas (SB 2420):** Enforceable since late May 2026, when the Fifth Circuit [paused](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/05/28/texas-apple-google-app-store-age-verification/) the December 2025 injunction. On June 4, 2026 it [extended that pause](https://www.ca5.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/25/25-51073-CV0.pdf) until the appeal is decided. In July 2026 the Supreme Court [declined to step in](https://www.scotusblog.com/2026/07/supreme-court-allows-texas-to-enforce-law-requiring-age-verification-and-parental-consent-on-app/), so the law is in force. Apple applies it to new Apple Accounts in Texas from June 4, 2026 ([Apple News](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=sg176nne)). Google verifies the ages of eligible users there ([Google docs](https://developer.android.com/distribute/age-appropriate-experiences/regional-protections)), and Play returns age signals for eligible users who created their accounts after May 28, 2026 ([0.0.4 docs](https://developer.android.com/google/play/age-signals/v4/overview)). The appeal itself was argued on August 4, 2026, and there's no decision yet. See [Issue #21](https://github.com/zigapovhe/age_range_signals/issues/21).
- **California (AB 1043):** From January 2027, sharing age ranges in California is mandatory on Play. [Google docs](https://developer.android.com/distribute/age-appropriate-experiences/regional-protections)
- **Alabama (HB 161):** Due to take effect January 1, 2027 ([source](https://www.alstonprivacy.com/louisiana-delays-app-store-accountability-effective-date-to-july-2027/)). Google lists the Play details as coming soon. [Google docs](https://developer.android.com/distribute/age-appropriate-experiences/regional-protections)
- **Utah and Louisiana:** The legal deadlines moved, but Apple already shares age categories for these users. Utah's ASAA now starts May 6, 2027 ([HB 498](https://www.wiley.law/wiley-connect/utah-amends-app-store-accountability-act-asaa-key-obligations-delayed-until-may-6-2027)). That bill also took away the attorney general's power to enforce it, so it can only be enforced through private lawsuits. Louisiana's now starts July 1, 2027 ([HB 977](https://www.alstonprivacy.com/louisiana-delays-app-store-accountability-effective-date-to-july-2027/)). Regardless of those dates, Apple shares age categories through DeclaredAgeRange for new Apple Accounts created in Utah since May 6, 2026 and in Louisiana since July 1, 2026. So `checkAgeSignals()` can return real data for those users today. [Apple News](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=f5zj08ey)
- **Apple Time Allowances (iOS 27):** Not a law, but an App Store requirement. Parents can manage time in Entertainment, Games and Social Media apps. Since September 2026, you have to say whether your app has social media features before you can submit a new version or update. Apps that do get at least a 13+ rating and go in the Social Media category for Time Allowances, which is separate from App Store categories. If you turn those features off for anyone under 13, the app stays out of that category for under-13s. But then Apple requires you to at least check age ranges with the Declared Age Range API, and a gate at 13 gives you that split. [Apple News](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=0d2gpmml)
